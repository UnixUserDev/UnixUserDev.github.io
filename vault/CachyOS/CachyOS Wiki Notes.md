## Notes on fidelity

- Each note carries the wiki page's own `updated:` date in frontmatter and the source URL.
- The wiki hides content behind **tabs** and **collapsible sections**; anything an automated fetch could not render was re-fetched from the raw `.mdx` source on `github.com/cachyos/wiki`, so commands here should match the live pages.
- Where a page genuinely didn't reveal something (e.g. the AMD→NVIDIA tab of the GPU migration guide), the note says so instead of guessing.
- Snapshots in time: ISO hashes, kernel variants and repo layouts change — re-check the wiki before acting on anything version-specific.
