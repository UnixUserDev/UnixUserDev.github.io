# Navigating the CachyOS Wiki

- The wiki is written for CachyOS-specific info, but much of it applies to Arch in general — cross-check the Arch Wiki (`https://wiki.archlinux.org/title/Main_page`) for extra context and detail.
- **Feedback**: every page has an `Edit page` button at the bottom linking straight to the file on GitHub; issues go to `github.com/CachyOS/wiki`.
- **Search first**: search bar at the top, right of the CachyOS logo.
- **Sidebars**: the left sidebar is the scrollable global table of contents; the right sidebar usually shows the current page's sections.
- **Interactive elements** — two traps when reading or scraping the wiki:
  - **Tabs** hold related content; check every tab, or you lose half the page.
  - **Expandable sections** (e.g. "Manual Installation (Advanced)") hide detail until clicked.
- Code blocks all have a `Copy to clipboard` button.
- **Pay attention to notes, tips, warnings and cautions** — the page calls them out as the place common pitfalls are documented.
- Theme selector (Dark/Light/Auto) and language selector live in the header. Non-English translations exist but lag the English pages.

> Practical takeaway: any automated extraction of this wiki that does not render tabs and expandables will silently miss content. That happened while building these notes — see the "Gap" callouts in this folder.
